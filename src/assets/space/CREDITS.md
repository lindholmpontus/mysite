# Texture credits

The planet, sun and Milky Way maps here are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (based on NASA
mission imagery). They're credited in the site's footer.

Changes made here: downscaled to 1024×512 and re-encoded (the Milky Way stays
2048×1024); Mars desaturated 20% toward its true-colour look; Earth's cloud
map kept as grayscale. `milky_way.jpg` keeps only the large-scale structure
of their panorama (blurred, point stars removed) with procedural star clouds,
dust lanes and colour grading. Saturn's ring is drawn as a CSS gradient
sampled from their ring map (collapsed to a 1D radial
profile and re-tinted).
